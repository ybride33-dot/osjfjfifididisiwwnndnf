# OP QR tools

Open OP, then expand QR 도구 above the travel tools. Enter text or a link and select 만들기; 이미지 저장 saves a PNG. For reading, choose a PNG/JPEG/WebP image (max 12 MiB and 24 million pixels) or tap 카메라 켜기 and allow access. Successful detection stops the camera and displays the decoded text; a link is never opened automatically, and the explicit open link is displayed only for HTTP/HTTPS URLs. Camera is stopped when the OP closes, the QR panel closes, the tab is hidden, or the user stops it. A delayed permission grant after close is also stopped. Clipboard permissions/browser support may affect the copy button; selection fallback is provided.

The encoder and decoder are local files in vendor/, precached by service worker on a successful v20.33 install. Thus both are available offline on first QR use after that installation; caches can be evicted and require an online update. The QR tool does not upload photos or QR text. Existing OP weather separately makes a network request when online. The QR code generator uses UTF-8, medium error correction, four-module quiet zone, and a maximum of 600 UTF-8 bytes; irregular barcodes, blurry photos, and non-UTF-8 QR content may not decode. Images are downscaled to 1280px for decoding and video to 720px; dense/small QR codes may fail. Camera requires permission, a supported secure browser context, and an available camera. Actual Galaxy A17 and offline device permissions have not been tested; mobile Chromium emulation and a simulated camera stream are not physical-camera proof.

`node tests/op-qr.test.js` checks codec round trips, Unicode and bounds. `python3 tests/op-qr-independent.test.py` additionally cross-checks four generated QR codes against OpenCV's decoder and three OpenCV-generated QR codes against jsQR (requires OpenCV and NumPy). `NODE_PATH=<playwright-node-modules> node tests/op-qr-browser.test.js` checks a v20.32 upgrade, first QR use offline, PNG download/photo reading, permission denial, fake camera reading, camera cleanup including delayed permission grants, offline reload and existing saved state.
